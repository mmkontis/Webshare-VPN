About speed tests

Tests download up to 5 MB from Cloudflare and check your exit IP with Webshare. Test all compares all 10 proxies and your browser default route, uses up to 55 MB, and restores your original route.

This measures download speed and HTTPS response latency through the selected route. Latency is the median of three small requests to Cloudflare, includes the network and server response, and is not ChatGPT model generation time. Reload open websites after changing the proxy or site settings. Local and private network addresses stay direct.
